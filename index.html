Chess Lens

  1. Set up the pieces in the starting position.
  2. Point the camera at the board so all 64 squares are in view.
  3. Wait for the cyan outline. It works out White's side and puts a1 on White's left corner. Wrong corner? Tap Rotate.
  4. It works out your colour (the pieces nearest you). Wrong? Tap You: to switch. Then tap Start.
  5. Play. On your turn the best move lights up green and is read out; on your opponent's turn it just watches. Keep the board in view so it sees every move.

Misread move? Undo. Keep the app open: the camera stops if you lock the phone.
